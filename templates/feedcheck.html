How to submit

  1. Apple — podcastsconnect.apple.com, sign in, Add Show → paste the feed URL. Apple emails the owner address above to verify you own it. Review usually takes a few days.
  2. Spotify — podcasters.spotify.com, Add your podcast → paste the same URL. Usually live within hours.
  3. Both keep polling the feed afterwards, so new episodes appear on their own. Nothing to do per episode.

Publishing an episode adds it to the feed; unpublishing removes it. Directories honour removals on their next poll, but a client that already downloaded an episode keeps its copy.
