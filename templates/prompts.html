Prompts

These are the instructions sent to the model. They are read at call time, so a save takes effect on the next episode — no redeploy, no restart. Edits are stored on the data volume; the shipped default stays available and every prompt can be reverted to it.

Changing these changes what every future episode sounds like. Existing episodes are untouched until you rewrite their scripts.
