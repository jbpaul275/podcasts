What these episodes are

Every episode on this site is generated by artificial intelligence. A language model reads a published academic paper and writes a script; a speech model performs it. The two hosts are synthetic. No one from {{ feed_title }} recorded them, and no human wrote the dialogue.

Episodes are published on an as-is basis and have not been independently fact-checked.

They may be wrong

AI-generated content can misstate findings, invent details, garble numbers, attribute claims to people who never made them, and omit the caveats that matter most. Some of these errors are difficult to detect by ear. Assume any given statement in an episode may be inaccurate.

Do not treat an episode as a reliable account of the paper it discusses. If a claim matters to you, read the original paper. Nothing here is professional, financial, legal, medical, or investment advice, and it should not be relied on in making any decision.
